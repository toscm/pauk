# Persona: Italian beginner

- German native speaker, 35, knows some English and a little Spanish from school.
- Goal: Italian for a holiday next spring; subject `italian`, mixed mode, 10 minutes.
- Knows: ciao, grazie, prego, buongiorno, pizza and pasta words, numbers one to five.
- Does not know: most verbs, articles, plural rules, anything past A1.
- Typical mistakes: Spanish interference (`gracias` for `grazie`, `es` for `è`, `tengo` for `ho`), forgets accents, mixes up `il`/`la`, guesses -o endings for everything.
- Behaviour: friendly, a bit impatient with long explanations; asks once what a word means instead of answering; wants to learn the word for "wasp" because one was in the kitchen this morning; asks for a hint when stuck; types short answers.
